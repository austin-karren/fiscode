The year-end packet is a printable summary plus a yearly CSV, intended as a handoff to a tax
professional. It is not a tax return.

## Contents

- **Profile snapshot** — filing status, state, active entity for the year, dependents.
- **Income totals** — by client, by source type (1099 vs W-2), by quarter.
- **Expense totals** — by category, with §179 candidates flagged.
- **Vehicle + mileage** — per-vehicle business miles, standard-mileage deduction.
- **Home office** — simplified-method calculation with the acknowledgement flag.
- **Tax estimate** — federal, state, SE, additional Medicare, QBI; quarterly plan and
  safe-harbor status.
- **`// todo: verify` registry** — every year-config constant that has not been verified against
  IRS/SSA for the active year, surfaced so your accountant can spot stale assumptions.

## Generating it

From `/year-end`, click **Download year-end packet**. The yearly CSV is filtered to records dated
inside the year plus the active singletons clipped to the year boundaries.
