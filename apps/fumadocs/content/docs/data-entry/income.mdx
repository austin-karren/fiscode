Edit at `/income`. `source_type` is `1099` or `w2`. Amounts are stored in integer cents. Entries
are dated so the quarterly engine can bucket them correctly.

## What counts toward the tax estimate

Only `1099` rows. `w2` rows are stored for record-keeping (and they show up in the year-end
packet), but the tax engine treats W-2 income on a `spouse` span — not on the individual income
rows — because the personal W-2 case is out of scope for v1.

## Kinds

The `kind` column is a sub-categorization for your own organization: `recurring`, `bonus`,
`consulting`, `other`. The estimate doesn't differentiate.

## CSV

CSV `record_type` = `income`. See the [CSV format reference](/docs/csv-format#income).
