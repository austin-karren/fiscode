title: Time
description: Time-tracking sessions.
---

Edit at `/time`. Time entries are independent of income — useful for hourly clients and for
quantifying effort by client/project at year end. **Not consumed by the tax engine.**

The estimate runs entirely off income entries. Time is here so you don't need a separate tool;
if you bill hourly, the year-end packet will summarize hours-per-client alongside the income
totals for cross-checking.

## CSV

CSV `record_type` = `time_entry`. See the [CSV format reference](/docs/csv-format#time_entry).
Duration is stored as `minutes` (integer).
