title: Mileage
description: Per-trip business miles.
---

Edit at `/mileage`. Each trip has a date, optional vehicle, business miles, and an optional
purpose. The tax engine sums all trips for the year and multiplies by the year's IRS standard
mileage rate (see [tax engine](/docs/tax-engine)).

## Rate by year

- **2025** — $0.70/mile (IRS Notice 2024-)
- **2026** — $0.725/mile (placeholder until IRS publishes the official rate)

Numbers are seeded in `packages/tax/src/config/{year}.ts`. If the remote tax-data mirror is
configured, it overrides the seed.

## SE-start gating

Trips dated before your `profile.se_start_date` are excluded from the deduction but stay in your
log. The dashboard surfaces an "excluded" count so the discrepancy isn't silent.

## CSV

CSV `record_type` = `mileage`. See the [CSV format reference](/docs/csv-format#mileage). The
`vehicle_id` column is optional — trips without one still count toward the deduction; they're
just not attributable to a specific vehicle in your records.
